> THE ADJECTIVE.
>
> An adjective modifies a noun or a pronoun.
>
> To modify means "to describe or to make more definite" the meaning of a word. Adjectives modify nouns or pronouns by telling what kind, which one, how many, or how much.
>
> #### <u>**EXAMPLES**</u>
>
>
> What Kind? – brown shoes / English tea / up-to-date fashion
>
> Which One? – those cars / first step / last one
>
> How Many? – ten boxes / several books / many students
>
> How Much? – some water / less time / enough money
>
> Adjectives usually precede the words they modify:
>
> **EXAMPLE:** The wild and graceful deer ran through the forest.
