> #### <u>**NOUNS OR ADVERBS?**</u>
>
>
> Some words may be used as nouns or as adverbs.
>
> **NOUN:** They returned to their home.
>
> **ADVERB:** They returned home before noon.
>
> **NOUN:** Yesterday was a good day.
>
> **ADVERB:** The teacher reviewed what had been covered yesterday.
