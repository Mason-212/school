*The most important sentance in any essay is your **thesis statement**. The thesis statement needs to thoroughly address the prompt that you are asked to respond to.* Whenever possible, I try to craft our writing prompts in the form of a question, and your thesis is your answer to that question. 

The thesis statement states your opinion and gives *a brief reason **how or why** your opnion is valid.*  A thesis statement needs to be clear and direct. You want your ideas to be complex in your thesis, *but you want to keep the sentence as short as. and direct to the point as possible.*
The supporting argument topic sentence ***"elaborates" on the reason how or why your opinion is valid.** this means your topic sentence claims will be more specific then your thesis.* If you think about the fact that your thesis is your argument for the whole essay, while your topic sentence is only the argument for that one paragraph, this should make sense.

*Your argument in your topic sentence **must support your thesis***. Since we will be writing four paragraph essays, you will be creating two supporting argument topic sentences for each thesis statement.

*The role of your supporting argument topic sentence is to summarize your assigment for your supporting argument(body) paragraph*. The *topic sentence is just like a mini-thesis for your supporting paragraph.* Once you have your topic sentence the rest of the paragraph will be introducing stating, and analyzing evidence that proves your cliam in your topic sentence and thesis.

Here is a sample complex thesis statement with a sample supporting argument topic sentence:
**Topic: Who is the best actor in modern day film? Thesis: Leonardo DiCaprio is the best actor today *because* of the range of characters he can successfully play.**

**Supporintng argument topic sentence: DiCaprio proved he was a master at winning the hearts of his audiences through his preformance in Titanic.**

The thesis argues the reason *why* Leonardo DiCaprio is the best actor is *because* of his ability to play different types of characters. *The body(support) for this essay would have to prove that he is successful at playing different types of characters*. The supporting argument paragraph for our sample topic sentence will argue that he is successful at playng the like able character.

*For a four paragraph essay the writer would need one more supporting argument topic sentence for his/her thesis statement*. Just so that you can see how the supporting argument topic sentences will blend with the thesis, I will show you the thesis and two supporting argument topic sentences in an outline form that you will be using when you prepare your essays:

**Thesis: Leonardo Di Caprio is the best actor today *because* of the range of characters that he can successfully play.**
**Supporting argument sentence #1: Early in his career, DiCaprio proved he was a master at winning the hearts of his audience through his performance in *Titanic***.

**Supporting argument topic sentence #2: In more recent roles, DiCaprio has been brilliant at playing the character that the audience doesn't know if they can trust as shown in his role in *The Departed***.

What is important to understand about *supporting argument topic sentences is that they extend the argument made in the thesis*. They have to be more specific than the argument in the thesis statement.

Finally, for each topic sentence (and body paragraph) we'll be including one direct quote from the text to use as evidence to prove your claim in your topic sentence.
