



> [!box]
> THE ADJECTIVE.
>
> An adjective modifies a noun or a pronoun.
>
> To modify means "to describe or to make more definite" the meaning of a word. Adjectives modify nouns or pronouns by telling what kind, which one, how many, or how much.
>
> #### <u>**EXAMPLES**</u>
>
>
> What Kind? – brown shoes / English tea / up-to-date fashion
>
> Which One? – those cars / first step / last one
>
> How Many? – ten boxes / several books / many students
>
> How Much? – some water / less time / enough money
>
> Adjectives usually precede the words they modify:
>
> **EXAMPLE:** The wild and graceful deer ran through the forest.


When using multiple adjectives to describe the same noun or pronoun, there is actually a preferred order. Here it is:

**EXAMPLE:** The wild and graceful deer ran through the forest.

The most frequently used adjectives are a, an, and the. These words are called articles. A and an are called indefinite articles because they refer to any member of a general group. A is used before a word beginning with a consonant sound. An is used before a word beginning with a vowel sound. The is called the definite article because it refers to someone or something in <u>particular.</u>

#### <u>**EXAMPLES**</u>


Jorge drew pictures of <u>a</u> pelican and <u>an</u> albatross.

For <u>an</u> hour I rode through the park in <u>a</u> horse-drawn carriage.

Maple Avenue is <u>a</u> one-way street.

#### <u>**Example**</u>


A word may be used as one part of speech in one context and as a different part of speech in another context. For example, the following words may be used as adjectives or as pronouns:
all / another / any / both / each / either / few / many / more / most / much / neither
/ one / other / several / some / such / that / these / this / those / what / which /
whose
Remember that an adjective <u>modifies</u> a noun or a pronoun and that a pronoun <u>takes the place</u> a noun or a pronoun.

#### <u>**EXAMPLES**</u>


**ADJECTIVE:** <u>Which</u> museum did you visit? [Which modifies the noun museum.]
**PRONOUN:** <u>Which</u> did you visit? [Which takes the place of the noun museum.]

Maple Avenue is a one-way street.

The lion is often called "the king of the beasts."
