

#### <u>**THE INTERJECTION**</u>


An interjection expresses emotion and has no grammatical relation to the rest of the sentence.

#### <u>**EXAMPLES**</u>


ah / aha / hey / oh / oops / ouch / uh-oh / well / whew / wow
An interjection is often set off from the rest of the sentence by an exclamation point or comma. An exclamation point indicates strong emotion. A comma indicates mild emotion.

Ouch! That hurts!

Well, I think you should apologize to her.
