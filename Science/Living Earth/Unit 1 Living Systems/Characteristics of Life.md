## Key Definitions for Characteristics of Life: 

**Growth & Development:**  Living things undergo organized Changes over time

**Reproduction**: Living systems derive new organisms directly or as part of a life cycle

**Reacting to Stimuli:** Living things detect and react to changes in the environment

**Metabolism:** Living things obtain and use energy and matter to carry the process Sas

**Homeostasis:** Living things regulate internal conditions within the environment range of life

**Cellular Organization:** Living things are made of one or more cells

**Genetic Information:** Living things store and transmit biological information that enables 
development
