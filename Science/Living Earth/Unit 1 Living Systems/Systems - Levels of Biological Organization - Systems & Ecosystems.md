


## Systems: Levels of Biological Organization — Systems & Ecosystems

System: A set of interacting components considered together.

| Components | Interactions                    | Boundary                             |
| ---------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| The parts  | relationships between the parts | What we choose to include or exclude |
|            |                                 |                                      |

- **Biotic** — living or once living organisms
- **Abiotic** — non-living physical and chemical components

**Ecosystem:** Organisms interacting with each other and with the nonliving environment.

*Diagram in notes: sun / plant (producer) / boundary, with arrows labeled “interaction.”*

**Key Idea:** A system is more than a list of parts. Interactions matter.

### Biological organization

cell → tissue → organ → organ system → organism → population → community → ecosystem → biome → biosphere

| Level | Working description | Chicken example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organism | one individual living thing | one developing chicken |
| Population | members of the same species in the same area | group of chickens at camp |
| Community (biotic) | all the populations of different species interacting | chicken, plants, insects, microbes |
| Ecosystem (biotic + abiotic) | community from above plus the abiotic | community, water, air, light |

### Questions

- **Organism:** How does the mass of the developing chicken change over time?
- **Population:** How does the mass change across the group of developing chickens?
- **Community:** What organisms do chickens interact with?
- **Ecosystem:** How do chickens interact with other organisms and the physical environment?

**Key Idea:** Changing scale changes what we can observe, measure, and ask.

### ==Four Spheres==

| ==Geosphere== | ==Hydrosphere== |
| --- | --- |
| Solid earth, rocks, minerals, mountains, geologic features, the floor. | All of Earth's water in liquid, solid, or gaseous form no matter the source. |
| ==Atmosphere== | ==Biosphere== |
| Gas surrounding the Earth like the air, made of nitrogen, oxygen, and carbon dioxide. | Living organisms on Earth like microbes, fungi, and plants/animals. |

Spheres can overlap. The boxes aren't completely separate boxes but overlapping. Ex: soil & water vapor.
