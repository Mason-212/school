## 1. Intercepts (shifted circle)

**Question:** Find the x-intercepts and y-intercepts of `x^2 + (y-3)^2 = 25`.

**Your answer:** `x-int (5,0); y-ints -2 or 8`

**Correct answer:** `x-ints (±4, 0); y-ints (0, -2) and (0, 8)`

**How to do it:** x-int: set `y = 0` → `x^2 + 9 = 25` → `x^2 = 16` → `x = ±4`. y-int: set `x = 0` → `(y-3)^2 = 25` → `y-3 = ±5` → `y = 8` or `y = -2`.

**Where you went wrong:** You used the radius 5 as an x-intercept. The center is at `(0, 3)`, so you have to substitute `y = 0` instead of reading ±5 off the equation.

## 2. Rational equation

**Question:** Solve `12/x - 3 = 4/(2x) - 1/2`. Check for extraneous solutions.

**Your answer:** `(4, 0)`

**Correct answer:** `x = 4`

**How to do it:** Ban `x = 0`. Multiply through by LCD `2x` to get `24 - 6x = 4 - x`. Then `20 = 5x`, so `x = 4`. It is not 0, so it stays.

**Where you went wrong:** The value 4 was right, but this is a one-variable equation. The answer is `x = 4`, not a point.

## 3. Consecutive odd integers

**Question:** The sum of three consecutive odd integers is 183. Find the three integers.

**Your answer:** `60, 61, and 62`

**Correct answer:** `59, 61, 63`

**How to do it:** Let the smallest be `n`. Then `n + (n+2) + (n+4) = 183` → `3n + 6 = 183` → `n = 59`.

**Where you went wrong:** You used consecutive integers (one even on each side of 61). Consecutive odds skip by 2, so both neighbors of 61 must be odd.

## 4. Mixture

**Question:** Mix 7 mL of 42% solution with 4 mL of another solution to make 11 mL of 51%. Find the other concentration.

**Your answer:** `56.75%`

**Correct answer:** `66.75%`

**How to do it:** `0.42(7) + 4x = 0.51(11)` → `2.94 + 4x = 5.61` → `4x = 2.67` → `x = 0.6675 = 66.75%`.

**Where you went wrong:** The setup is the amount-of-solute equation; the 56.75 is 10 points low (2.27/4 instead of 2.67/4, or a slip after subtracting the 42% amount).

## 1. Distance

**Question:** Find the distance between `(1, -2)` and `(4, 2)`.

**Your answer:** `4/3`

**Correct answer:** `5`

**How to do it:** `√[(4-1)^2 + (2-(-2))^2] = √(9+16) = √25 = 5`.

## 2. Even/odd

**Question:** Is `f(x) = x^3 - 4x` even, odd, or neither? Name the symmetry.

**Your answer:** `neither, no symmetry`

**Correct answer:** `odd; origin symmetry`

**How to do it:** `f(-x) = -x^3 + 4x = -(x^3 - 4x) = -f(x)`, so odd. Opposite x with opposite y is origin symmetry.

**Where you went wrong:** You have to compute `f(-x)` and compare it to `f(x)` and `-f(x)`. This one matches `-f(x)`.

**Question:** Find the x-intercepts and y-intercepts of `x^2 + (y-1)^2 = 17`.

**Your answer:** `x-int (√17, 0); y-int (0, √17)`

**How to do it:** y=0 → `x^2 + 1 = 17` → `x = ±4`. x=0 → `(y-1)^2 = 17` → `y = 1 ± √17`.

**Where you went wrong:** Same as the earlier circle: you treated `√17` like intercepts of a circle at the origin. Substitute `y=0` and `x=0`.

## 5. Complementary angles

**Question:** Two complementary angles are in the ratio `2:3`. Find both measures.

**Your answer:** `18 and 54`

**Correct answer:** `36° and 54°`

**How to do it:** Complementary means sum 90. `2x + 3x = 90` → `5x = 90` → `x = 18`. The angles are `2x = 36` and `3x = 54`.

**Where you went wrong:** 18 is the part `x`, not an angle. The 2-part is `36`, not `18`. (18+54=72, not 90.)

## 1. Intercepts (shifted circle)

**Question:** Find the x-intercepts and y-intercepts of `(x - 2)^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 25`.

**Your answer:** `x-ints (-4, 0) and (6, 0); y-ints (-4, 0) and (6, 0)`

**How to do it:** x-int: set `y = 0` → `(x - 2)^2 + 1 = 25` → `(x - 2)^2 = 24` → `x = 2 ± 2√6`. y-int: set `x = 0` → `4 + (y + 1)^2 = 25` → `(y + 1)^2 = 21` → `y = -1 ± √21`.

**Where you went wrong:** You used the left/right endpoints through the circle's center instead of substituting `y = 0`. For y-intercepts, the x-coordinate must be `0`.

## 2. Intercepts (shifted circle)

**Question:** Find the x-intercepts and y-intercepts of `(x + 3)^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 16`.

**Your answer:** `x-ints (-3 ± 2√3, 0); y-ints (2 ± √7, 0)`

**Correct answer:** `x-ints (-3 - 2√3, 0) and (-3 + 2√3, 0); y-ints (0, 2 - √7) and (0, 2 + √7)`

**How to do it:** x-int: set `y = 0` → `(x + 3)^2 + 4 = 16` → `(x + 3)^2 = 12` → `x = -3 ± 2√3`. y-int: set `x = 0` → `9 + (y - 2)^2 = 16` → `(y - 2)^2 = 7` → `y = 2 ± √7`.

**Where you went wrong:** The x-values were right, but the y-intercepts were written as x-intercepts. A y-intercept must have the form `(0, y)`.

## 1. Current / rate problem

**Question:** A boat travels `48 miles` downstream in `3 hours`. The return trip upstream takes `6 hours`. Find the speed of the boat in still water and the speed of the current.

**Your answer:** `16 mph downstream; 8 mph upstream`

**Correct answer:** `boat speed 12 mph; current speed 4 mph`

**How to do it:** Downstream rate is `48/3 = 16`; upstream rate is `48/6 = 8`. Let boat speed be `b` and current be `c`: `b + c = 16`, `b - c = 8`. Add to get `2b = 24`, so `b = 12`; then `c = 4`.

**Where you went wrong:** You found the downstream and upstream rates, but the question asked for boat speed in still water and current speed.

---

## 2. Age problem

**Question:** A father is 3 times as old as his son. Ten years ago, the father was 5 times as old as his son. How old are they now?

**Your answer:** `15 and 45`

**Correct answer:** `son 20; father 60`

**How to do it:** Let son be `x`, father be `3x`. Ten years ago: `3x - 10 = 5(x - 10)`. Then `3x - 10 = 5x - 50` → `40 = 2x` → `x = 20`, so father is `60`.

**Where you went wrong:** Your ages matched the current `3 times` relationship, but not the “ten years ago” relationship.
