
## The Printing Press:
- Invented in 1450 by Johann Gutenburg in Mainz Germany
- Books used to be handmade by hand by monks
- Helped spread Ideas of who should rule, how they should rule, and the individual rights spread with the printing press
- Helped challenge the rulers of the day
- During Guten Burgs time the world was based on empires controlled by a singular monarch or family. Authority was based on family, not how they ruled. 
- Printing press helped people become more literate and read different thinkers, ideas, how to govern and rule(even Biblical **teachings** ) and the Societies they lived in
