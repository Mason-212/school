# Democracy Definitions:

a system of government by the whole population or all the eligible members of a state, typically through elective representatives

is a form of government in which **the people** have the **authority** to choose their **officials** to **deliberate and decide** legislation via direct democracy

  

**Democracy itself means *rule of the people*****

A democracy is a system where people can change their rulers in a peaceful manner and the government is given the right to rule because the people will it
